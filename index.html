1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Milbul (מילבול) ("the app", "we") handles information when you use our Android application, an offline Hebrew word-search game. We keep data collection to a minimum.

2. Data We Collect

The App Itself Collects No Personal Data

Milbul does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on our servers. We operate no servers.

  • The dictionary and the word definitions are bundled inside the app. The game works without a connection.
  • Your current board, found words, statistics, daily-board history, settings and purchase status are stored locally on your device, in the app's private storage. They never leave your device.
  • The app has no accounts, no login, no registration, and no analytics of its own.
  • The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location, storage or phone-state permissions.

What Third-Party SDKs Collect

The app includes Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display ads, and Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent where the law requires it. The Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects:

This data is collected and sent to Google automatically by the SDK over an encrypted connection. It is the only data the app transmits off your device. Milbul itself makes no other network requests.

Google Play Billing handles all in-app purchases. We do not receive or store your payment card information. Google's terms apply: https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/

Wiktionary Links

Word definitions are shown inside the app from the bundled data. If you tap "Open in Wiktionary", the page opens in your browser, and that website's own privacy policy applies.

4. Advertising, Consent and Opt-Out

The app shows three kinds of ads through Google AdMob: an optional rewarded video you can watch to get a hint, an occasional full-screen ad after a finished board, and a small banner on the statistics and settings screens. Ads are filtered to a general (G) content rating, and the app is not directed at children.

Where required (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and some US states), the app asks for your consent before requesting ads. If a privacy choice applies to you, a Privacy options entry appears in the app's Settings so you can change your choice at any time.

To opt out of personalized ads on your device:

Android 12+: Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID
Android 7-11: Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization

5. In-App Purchases

Two one-time purchases are available. Prices are shown in your local currency in the app and in Google Play.

  • Remove Ads - removes the full-screen and banner ads. The optional rewarded hint video stays available.
  • Plus - everything in Remove Ads, plus unlimited hints without videos and additional colour themes.

All transactions are processed by Google Play. Refunds are handled by Google Play under its terms and applicable law.

6. Children's Privacy

Milbul is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app targets a general audience (13+).

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Because the app stores its data locally on your device:

  • To delete all app data: Settings > Apps > Milbul > Storage > Clear data, or uninstall the app. You can also reset your statistics from the app's Statistics screen. Purchases are tied to your Google account and are restored by Google Play.
  • To delete ad-related identifiers: reset your Advertising ID (see Section 4).

8. Security

Network communication by the ads SDK and Google Play uses HTTPS. We store no personal data on any server because we operate none.
